2. Food Types, Power-ups & Special Items

Eating items feeds your snake, increases length, awards points, and activates unique power-up status effects.

  • Normal Apple (+10 Points)

    Standard food item. Increases snake length by +1 segment and contributes towards level progression.

  • Golden Bonus (+30 Base Points)

    Spawns randomly with a 5-second countdown ring. Awards 3x standard score points if collected before expiration.

  • Speed Boost Berry (+15 Points)

    Increases snake speed by +20% for 6 seconds while granting a +5 bonus to all active combo multiplier streaks.

  • Ghost Shield Power-Up

    Allows the snake to phase through its own body tail and boundary walls safely for 5 seconds without triggering a crash.

4. Game Modes, Skins & Daily Quests

1

Wall Collision vs Wrap-Around

In classic mode, touching perimeter walls results in Game Over. Toggle wall wrap-around in Settings to allow passing through walls to opposite sides.

2

Rival AI Bot Mode

Enable AI Rival Bot in settings to spawn an autonomous computer snake running real-time A* pathfinding. Compete to collect food items first!

3

Custom Cosmetic Skins

Unlock vibrant snake skins (Neon Cyan, Retro Arcade, Golden Monarch, Crimson Dragon) by reaching target high scores or finishing Daily Quests.

5. Pro Strategies for High Score Domination

1

Serpentine Coiling Pattern

Guide your snake in tight horizontal or vertical 'S' patterns. This keeps your tail structured cleanly behind you and leaves max open grid space.

2

Perimeter Wall Wrapping

Early in the game, circle along the outer edge of the grid. This reserves the inner tile matrix for newly spawning food items and power-ups.

3

Emergency Tail Evasion

Never trap yourself inside a closed loop. Always calculate an escape path towards your moving tail tip before diving into tight spaces.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Type in the search box below or toggle accordions to find quick solutions.

How do I control the snake on desktop and mobile?

On desktop, use Arrow Keys (Up, Down, Left, Right) or WASD to navigate. Press Space or P to pause or resume. On mobile devices, use touch swipes anywhere on screen or on-screen directional touch controls.

How does the scoring system and combo multiplier work?

Each food item awards base points. Eating food items in quick succession within 3.5 seconds triggers a combo multiplier (up to 5x boost). Special gold foods award 3x points, while speed power-ups boost your speed and extra bonus points.

How do I save my high score on the leaderboard?

When your game ends, if your score ranks in the top 10, a prompt will automatically allow you to enter your name and select an avatar badge to save to the leaderboard in local storage.

Does Modern Snake work offline as a Progressive Web App (PWA)?

Yes! Modern Snake includes an integrated Service Worker that caches all game assets, styles, scripts, and guides. You can install it on your mobile device or desktop home screen and play offline anytime.

Why is there no audio playing on mobile devices?

Mobile web browsers require an initial user tap or button press to unlock Web Audio API audio context initialization. Simply tap the sound toggle button once after starting a game to enable zero-asset procedural retro sound FX.

How do I unlock custom snake skins and cosmetics?

Skins are unlocked automatically as you achieve specific high score milestones (e.g. 500 pts, 1,000 pts), reach higher levels, or complete daily quests in the game.

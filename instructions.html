2. Food Types, Power-ups & Special Items

Consuming food items feeds your snake, increases length, awards points, and triggers dynamic status effects.

  • Normal Blue Apple (+10 Points)

    Standard food item. Increases snake length by +1 segment and contributes to level progression.

  • Golden Bonus Apple (+30 Base Points)

    Spawns with a 5-second expiration countdown ring. Awards 3x standard score points if collected before time runs out.

  • Speed Boost Berry (+15 Points)

    Increases snake movement speed by +20% for 6 seconds while granting a +5 bonus to all active combo streaks.

  • Ghost Shield Power-Up

    Allows your snake to phase safely through its own tail body and boundary walls for 5 seconds without crashing.

4. Game Modes, Skins & Daily Quests

1

Classic Wall Collision vs Wrap-Around

In classic mode, hitting border walls triggers Game Over. Toggle wall wrap-around mode in Settings to allow passing through walls to opposite sides.

2

Rival AI Bot Mode

Enable AI Rival Bot in settings to spawn a computer snake executing real-time A* pathfinding. Compete to grab apples first!

3

Custom Cosmetic Skins

Unlock snake skins (Neon Cyan, Retro Arcade, Golden Monarch, Crimson Dragon) by reaching high-score thresholds or finishing Daily Quests.

5. Pro Strategies for High Scores

1

Serpentine Coiling Pattern

Guide your snake in tight horizontal or vertical 'S' loops. This structures your tail cleanly behind you and leaves maximum open grid space.

2

Perimeter Wall Wrapping

Early in the round, circle along outer grid edges. This reserves the inner tile matrix for newly spawning food items and power-ups.

3

Emergency Tail Evasion

Never trap yourself inside a closed loop. Always calculate an escape path towards your moving tail tip before entering tight spaces.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Type in the search box below or toggle accordions to find answers.

How do I control the snake on desktop and mobile?

On desktop, use Arrow Keys (Up, Down, Left, Right) or WASD to navigate. Press Space or P to pause or resume. On mobile devices, use touch swipes anywhere on screen or on-screen directional touch controls.

How does the scoring system and combo multiplier work?

Each food item awards base points. Eating food items in quick succession within 3.5 seconds triggers a combo multiplier (up to 5x boost). Special gold foods award 3x points, while speed power-ups boost your speed and extra bonus points.

How do I save my high score on the leaderboard?

When your game ends, if your score ranks in the top 10, a prompt automatically lets you enter your name and select an avatar badge to save to the leaderboard in local storage.

Does Modern Snake work offline as a Progressive Web App (PWA)?

Yes! Modern Snake includes an integrated Service Worker caching all game assets, styles, scripts, and guides. You can install it on your mobile device or desktop home screen and play offline anytime.

Why is there no audio playing on mobile devices?

Mobile web browsers require an initial user tap or button press to unlock Web Audio API audio context initialization. Tap the sound toggle button once after starting a game to enable zero-asset procedural retro sound FX.

How do I unlock custom snake skins and cosmetics?

Skins are unlocked automatically as you achieve specific high score milestones (e.g. 500 pts, 1,000 pts), reach higher levels, or complete daily quests in the game.

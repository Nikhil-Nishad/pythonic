Food Types & Power-ups

  • Normal Apple (+10 pts)

    Standard food item. Consuming increases length and contributes to level progress.

  • Golden Bonus (+30 pts)

    Appears temporarily! Consuming grants 3x base multiplier points.

  • Speed Boost (+15 pts)

    Temporarily increases snake movement speed and gives bonus point boosts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I control the snake on desktop and mobile?

On desktop, use Arrow Keys (Up, Down, Left, Right) or WASD to navigate. Press Space to pause or resume. On mobile devices, use intuitive touch swipes or on-screen directional touch controls.

How does the scoring system and combo multiplier work?

Each food item awards base points. Eating food items in quick succession triggers a combo multiplier (up to 5x boost). Special gold foods award 3x points, while speed power-ups boost your speed and extra bonus points.

How do I save my high score on the leaderboard?

When your game ends, if your score ranks in the top 10, a prompt will let you enter your name and select an avatar badge to save to the leaderboard.

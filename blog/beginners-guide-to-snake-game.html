The Ultimate Beginner's Guide to Snake Games: Controls, Rules & Fun Tricks for Everyone

New to Snake? Learn movement controls, food types, scoring formulas, and simple tricks to stay alive longer and score high.

MS
Player Onboarding Team Published 2026-08-13
• ⏱️ 5 min read •

1. Welcome to the World of Snake!

Whether you're playing your very first game or revisiting Snake after many years, the core concept is easy to learn and endlessly fun: steer your snake around the screen to eat apples, grow longer, and avoid crashing!

2. The 3 Simple Golden Rules

  1. Eat Food to Grow: Each apple (+10 points) increases your score and adds 1 segment to your snake's length.
  2. Don't Crash: Hitting the border walls (in Classic mode) or bumping into your own tail ends the round.
  3. Build Combos: Eat apples quickly within 3.5 seconds to build up a 2x, 3x, or 5x score multiplier!

3. Controls for Desktop & Mobile

On desktop computers, use the Arrow Keys (Up, Down, Left, Right) or WASD keys to turn. On phones and tablets, simply swipe your finger in the direction you want to move!

4. Easy Beginner Tricks for Long Rounds

  • Stick to the Edges: Circle around the outer perimeter of the grid early in the game to keep the center wide open.
  • Avoid Double-Backing: Never try to make a 180-degree turn directly into your own neck!
  • Take a Breather: Need a pause? Press the Spacebar or tap the Pause button anytime.

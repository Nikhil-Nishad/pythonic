# 05. Google Play Store ASO & SEO Master Guide

App Store Optimization (ASO) is the mobile equivalent of SEO. This guide details the keyword algorithm, metadata copy, keyword density rules, and conversion rate optimization (CRO) strategies to rank on the first page of Google Play Store search results.

---

## 1. Google Play Store Ranking Algorithm (2026)

Google Play indexes and ranks apps based on two core signal categories:

```
                  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐

               ┌─────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────┐
```

## 5. Review & Rating Velocity Strategy

1. **Trigger In-App Review API at Peak Dopamine Moments**:
   - Never prompt on Game Over or app launch.
   - Prompt when player unlocks a new skin or beats their personal High Score.
2. **Review Keyword Seeding**:
   - Users naturally mention keywords if the app prompts: *"Enjoying the 60FPS classic snake action? Leave a quick review!"*
3. **Reply to All 1-Star and 2-Star Reviews within 24 Hours**:
   - Google algorithm rewards developer responsiveness with higher keyword ranking stability.

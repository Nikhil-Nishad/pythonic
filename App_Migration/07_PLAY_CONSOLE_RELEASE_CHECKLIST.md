# 07. Google Play Console Production Release Runbook

This checklist guides you step-by-step from zero to a live published app on the Google Play Store, including the 20-tester requirement, privacy policy, and Data Safety disclosures.

---

## 1. Pre-Flight Preparation Checklist

- [ ] **Google Play Developer Account**: Registered at [play.google.com/console](https://play.google.com/console) ($25 one-time fee).
- [ ] **Release Keystore**: Created and backed up securely (`release-key.jks`).
- [ ] **GitHub Actions Secrets**: Configured with keystore base64 and passwords.
- [ ] **Signed App Bundle (`.aab`)**: Downloaded from GitHub Actions build artifacts.
- [ ] **Visual Assets Ready**: 512x512 Icon, 1024x500 Feature Graphic, and 4+ Screenshots.
- [ ] **Privacy Policy URL**: Hosted on web (e.g. `https://pythonics.vercel.app/privacy.html`).

---

## 2. Setting Up the App in Play Console

1. In Play Console, click **Create App**.
2. **App details**:
   - App Name: `Modern Snake`
   - Default Language: `English (United States)`
   - App or Game: `Game`
   - Free or Paid: `Free`
3. **Declarations**:
   - Accept Developer Program Policies and US export laws.
   - Click **Create app**.

---

## 3. App Content & Data Safety Declarations

Complete all sections under **Policy** > **App content**:

### 3.1. Privacy Policy
- Provide URL: `https://pythonics.vercel.app/` (or create a dedicated `privacy.html` stating that no personal data is collected or transmitted).

### 3.2. Data Safety Questionnaire
Answer as follows (for this zero-tracking local arcade game):
- **Does your app collect or share any user data?** -> **`No`**
- *Result*: Your app displays the badge: *"No data collected. No third-party data sharing. Data is encrypted in transit."*

### 3.3. Ads Declaration
- Select: **`No, my app does not contain ads`** (Unless you integrate AdMob later).

### 3.4. Target Audience & Content Rating (IARC)
- **Target Age**: `13 and older` (Avoid 12-and-under to bypass strict COPPA family policy verification).
- **IARC Questionnaire**:
  - Category: `Game`
  - Violence / Blood: `No`
  - Fear / Horror: `No`
  - Sexuality: `No`
  - Simulated Gambling: `No`
  - Offensive Language: `No`
  - Result: **ESRB: Everyone (E)** / **PEGI: 3** (All ages).

### 3.5. Financial Features & Government Apps
- Select: **`No financial features`** and **`Not a government app`**.

## 4. The 20-Tester Closed Testing Requirement (Personal Accounts)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Since late 2023, Google requires all new personal developer accounts to run a **Closed Test with at least 20 opted-in testers for 14 continuous days** before requesting production access.

### How to complete the 20-tester requirement effortlessly:
1. **Create a Closed Testing Track**:
   - In Play Console, go to **Testing** > **Closed testing**.
   - Create a track named `Alpha Testing`.
2. **Create an Email List / Google Group**:
   - Create a Google Group (e.g. `modern-snake-testers@googlegroups.com`) or enter 20+ Gmail addresses of friends, family, or developer testing communities (such as Reddit `r/AndroidClosedTesting` or Indie Hackers).
3. **Upload Release `.aab`**:
   - Upload the signed `.aab` artifact generated by GitHub Actions.
   - Add Release Notes: `Initial Alpha build with 60FPS canvas game engine and A* AI rival mode.`
4. **Distribute Opt-In Link**:
   - Send the web opt-in link (`https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.nikhilnishad.modernsnake`) to all 20 testers.
   - Ensure all 20 testers click **"Become a Tester"** and install the app.
5. **Maintain 14 Days of Opt-In**:
   - Keep the testers opted in for 14 days. Prompt 2–3 testers to open the app every few days so Google's automated telemetry registers active engagement.
6. **Apply for Production Access**:
   - On day 15, click **Apply for production access** in the Play Console dashboard. Answer the simple questions about tester feedback and release quality. Google typically approves production access within 24–48 hours!

---

## 5. Production Launch

Once production access is granted:
1. Navigate to **Release** > **Production**.
2. Click **Create new release**.
3. Select the latest validated `.aab` from your Closed Testing track.
4. Review the release rollout (recommend starting with a `20% Staged Rollout` for the first 24 hours).
5. Click **Start rollout to Production**!
6. Google review takes 1–3 business days. Once approved, **Modern Snake is officially live worldwide on the Google Play Store!** 🚀
